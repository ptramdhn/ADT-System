# PRODUCT.md - PT Anugrah Djaya Tunggal (ADT-System)

## Purpose & Target Audience
ADT-System is a specialized Operations, Quotations (SPH), Purchase Order (PO), and Financial Realization platform for PT Anugrah Djaya Tunggal (mechanical fabrication, industrial spare parts, precision machining).
- **Primary Users**: Management, Sales/Estimator, Operations, and Project Partners (Karyawan 1 & Karyawan 2).
- **Core Jobs-to-be-Done**:
  1. Fast SPH quotation drafting via natural language AI extraction.
  2. Automatic PO conversion and client PDF ingestion.
  3. Real-time HPP project expense tracking (materials, machining, shipping).
  4. Instant transparent profit split (25% Karyawan 1, 25% Karyawan 2, 50% Kas PT ADT).
  5. Withdrawal ledger for employee payouts.
  6. Grounded anti-hallucination database Q&A assistant.
