# DESIGN.md - Impeccable Design System

## Design Philosophy & Identity
- **Visual Style**: Clean Modern SaaS (Inspired by Ehsan Moin Order Management Reference & Linear).
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Electric Cobalt Blue (#0062ff, #0052e0)
  - Canvas: Pure White (#ffffff) & Soft Slate/Ice Background (#eef3f9)
  - Accent / Meaning:
    - Success: Emerald Green (#10b981, #059669)
    - Expense / Danger: Coral Rose (#e11d48, #f43f5e)
    - Process / Warning: Amber (#f59e0b, #d97706)
- **Typography**:
  - Primary Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Friendly, confident, crisp geometric sans)
  - Monospace Font: JetBrains Mono (For currency figures, PO numbers, dates)
- **Responsive Principles**:
  - Mobile (<768px): Sticky mobile topbar + slide-in drawer sidebar + stacked touch cards.
  - Tablet (768px - 1024px): Collapsible/compact sidebar + 2-column grid.
  - Desktop (>1024px): Full floating card canvas with signature curved cutout active tabs.
